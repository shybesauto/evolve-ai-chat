    How to Play ❓

    👥Meet your team

    You have 5 AI teammates. Each is good at a different job — design, marketing, customer help, money, and big plans.

    💬Chat with them

    Tap a teammate and ask anything about your shop. They'll give you real ideas! Every chat earns XP.

    🧑‍💼Give them jobs

    Press "Assign Job" to put a teammate to work on something. Their status changes to show what they're doing.

    🎯Do missions

    The Mission Board gives you challenges. Finish them to earn coins and level up your empire.

    ⭐Level up

    Earn XP from chatting and missions. Fill the bar to reach the next level. How high can you go?

    🛟Stay safe

    Your teammates never ask for private info. For anything that costs money, always ask a parent first!

    👋 Note for parents: The AI teammates are powered by OpenAI and have built-in kid-safety rules. Set your OPENAI_API_KEY in Vercel to turn them on. See the README for the 5-minute setup.
